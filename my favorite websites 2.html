My Favorite Websites

ESPN

as a person who watches multiple sports like soccer, football, baseball and basketball. ESPN is not only a great source to check out scores for multiple sports, but also a great website to catch up with sports news and trades. This website also has live digital visual trackers for all sports, allowing people to follow the game with zero cable subscriptions or streaming fees, also, the gamecast has more advanced details compared to standard broadcasts, including basketball shot locations or baseball pitch types.

CHESS.COM

for the past few years, I have developed a huge interest in chess. It not only is a very interesting and fun boardgame, but also a game that trains your brain to think of stratigies and picture moves ahead. Chess.com is the most popular chess website in the world, it not only allows online gameplay between random people(which has a ton of different modes), it also has lots of other functions like puzzels, training, gamenwatching, analyzing. Making it my personal favorite chess web. I usually play around 3~4 games each day and analyze them afterwards.

CNN

I like to know what is going on in the world I live in, and CNN is the main source I get my news. The articles are all completely free, while the language is simple and not hard to understand. I try to read around 2 ariticles a day, most of them aren't too long.

MLB

Even though ESPN gives a clear visual tracker to baseball games, the mlb website is even better for baseball. It has more detailed stats, information, news, standings etc which all are in a better format. As a massive baseball fan, this is my go-to website to check news and scores(another reason I use the MLB website more is because I bought MLBTV lol). The MLB website also allows you to follow teams and players, making sure you never miss any news about your team and players. Recently, the MLB gamecast also added AI stats, which calculates much more advanced numbers for hitters and pitchers.

Instagram

Instagram is the main website/app I use to chat and keep in touch with my friends and family. It is also a great platform where I can share my life with other people.
